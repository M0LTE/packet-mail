Your data

Delete your Packet Mail data

Packet Mail, by Tom Fanning (M0LTE), keeps almost nothing about you. Here is how to remove what there is.

Data on your phone

Your callsign, connections, passwords, mail, bulletins and address book are stored only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall Packet Mail, or remove a connection in Settings to delete its mail. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Problem reports you sent

The only data that ever leaves your phone for the developer is a problem report, and only if you chose to send one. A report holds your description, the app and phone software versions, your BBS's version line, and up to 15 minutes of traffic with your BBS (never your password, and other people's message text only if you ticked the box).

To have your reports deleted:

  1. Email app-support@tomfanning.name with the subject "Packet Mail: delete my data".
  2. Say roughly when you sent the reports and the callsign you use in the app, so we can find them.

We delete every matching report, including its traffic file, within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept afterwards. Reports are otherwise kept only as long as they are needed to fix the problem they describe.

Your BBS

Mail and bulletins on your BBS belong to that BBS, not to Packet Mail. To have them removed, ask the BBS's sysop.
