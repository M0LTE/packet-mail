Mail held for want of an address can now be fixed in place: tap it in Outbox, add the home BBS (tap a suggestion or Look up), and it goes at once. Held mail is also tried again at every sync, asking White Pages on all your BBSes. Fixed sign-in for accounts that start the BBS by themselves, which made replies arrive one step late and White Pages look-ups fail. Callsigns now show a slashed zero, so M0LTE no longer reads as MOLTE.
