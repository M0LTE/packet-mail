Mail held for want of an address can now be fixed in place: tap it in Outbox, add the home BBS (tap a suggestion or Look up), and it goes at once. Each sync also checks held mail against White Pages on that BBS, so it goes by itself once any of your BBSes knows the address. Fixed sign-in for accounts that start the BBS by themselves, which made White Pages look-ups fail. Callsigns now show a slashed zero, so M0LTE no longer reads as MOLTE.
