# Packet Mail privacy policy

Packet Mail does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**What stays on your phone.** Your callsign, name and home BBS, your connections, your mail and bulletins, and your address book are kept only on your phone. Passwords are kept in the phone's secure storage. The app talks only to the BBS you set up, at the address you give it.

**Problem reports.** If you choose "Report a problem", the app prepares a report with your description, the app and phone software versions, and the last 15 minutes of traffic with your BBS. Passwords are never included, and other people's message text is left out unless you tick the box to include it. You see exactly what will be sent before anything leaves your phone, and it is sent from your own email app, to the developer, only when you send it.

**Amateur radio.** Packet radio mail is not private: messages travel unencrypted and may be read by anyone along the way.
