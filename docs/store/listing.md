# Store listing (draft)

**Name:** Packet Mail

**Subtitle (30 characters):** Packet radio BBS mail

**Promotional text:** Your packet BBS mail, in a proper mail app.

**Description:**

Packet Mail brings packet radio BBS mail to your phone with the ease of an ordinary email app.

Add a connection to your BBS the way you'd add an email account, and your personal mail and bulletins appear in an inbox you already know how to use. Read on the go, reply, write to several stations at once, and keep an address book that fills itself in as mail arrives.

- Inbox, Outbox and Sent, with each message's forwarding progress
- A bulletins browser grouped by category and area, with subscriptions
- Hierarchical addresses checked as you type, and home BBS lookups through White Pages
- Read messages exactly as sent, or in a comfortable reflowed view
- Works with LinBPQ (BPQMail) and XRouter over your network

Packet Mail is for licensed radio amateurs. It talks to your own BBS over your local network, or over a VPN such as Tailscale when you're away. It collects no data.
